Prez Network

Prez Network is a clipping channel on TikTok that shares short highlights from livestreams.

The Prez Network app is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It prepares clips from recordings the owner is authorized to use, and publishes them to the owner's own Prez Network TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

Original creators keep all rights to their content. If you are a creator and want a clip changed or removed, use the contact details in our Privacy Policy.
