Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Who this covers

Prez Network is a private tool used only by the owner of the Prez Network TikTok account. Nobody else signs in to it, and it collects no personal information from TikTok viewers or the public.

What data the app uses

  • The owner's TikTok connection: when the owner connects their account, TikTok provides access tokens, the account's basic profile (username, display name, avatar), follower statistics, and statistics for the account's own videos. These are used only to publish the owner's videos and show how they perform.
  • Public streaming information: public channel details from Twitch and Kick (such as whether a channel is live and its public past broadcasts), retrieved through their official APIs.
  • Video files: recordings the owner is authorized to use, and the clips made from them.

Where it is stored

All data is stored locally on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or sent to any other service, except to TikTok, Twitch and Kick when the app calls their official APIs.

How long it is kept

Data is kept only as long as the owner needs it for the channel. Disconnecting TikTok in the app deletes the stored TikTok tokens, and the owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings.
