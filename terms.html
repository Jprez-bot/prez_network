Terms of Service

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. What Prez Network is

Prez Network ("we", "the app") is a private, single-user tool run by the owner of the Prez Network TikTok account. It helps the owner find, edit, schedule and publish short clips to that account. It is not a public service: there are no customer accounts, and no one other than the owner can sign in or use it.

2. Use of TikTok

The app connects only to the owner's own TikTok account, and only after the owner approves the connection on TikTok's official sign-in page. It uses TikTok's official APIs to publish videos the owner has reviewed and to read performance statistics for the owner's own posts. Every use follows TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Developer Terms. The owner can disconnect the app at any time, in the app or in TikTok's settings.

3. Content and rights

Clips are made only from recordings the owner has permission to use. Each clip is reviewed by a person before it is published. Creators keep all rights to their original content, and clips include source attribution where appropriate. We don't remove watermarks to hide a source or present others' work as our own.

4. Requests from creators

If you are a creator and want a clip corrected or removed, contact us (see the Privacy Policy). We handle valid requests promptly.

5. No warranty

The app is provided "as is" for the owner's personal use, with no guarantee of availability or results.

We may update these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version.
